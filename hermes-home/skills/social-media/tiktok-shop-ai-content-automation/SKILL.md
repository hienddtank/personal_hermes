# TikTok Shop AI Content Automation — Research & Workflow Guide

Use this skill when building content pipelines for e-commerce sellers, especially food/snack brands on TikTok Vietnam. Covers competitor intelligence, AI video generation, and practical automation workflows.

Before creating content, find what's already generating sales:

## 3. Recommended Workflow (4 Steps)

**Step 1: Spy & Discover** (Daily, 15 min)
- FastMoss or TikTok Creative Center → filter by category
- Note top 3 trending videos of the day (hooks, formats, captions)

**Step 2: Generate Ideas** (Weekly, 1 hour batch)
- Feed trending videos to ChatGPT/Claude:
> "I sell [product] in [market]. Here are 5 viral videos performing well: [details]. Extract content patterns, hooks used, emotional triggers. Generate 20 original video ideas for my brand."

**Step 3: Create Content** (Daily, 1–2 hours)
- ASMR: Film with phone + external mic. No AI — authenticity matters. CapCut for subtitles.
- Product showcases: Upload photos to CreatOK → dynamic videos in seconds.
- BTS/educational: Real kitchen footage (can't be faked). CapCut AI auto-captions.
- Trend participation: Use trending audio from TikTok Creative Center, adapt hook to product.

**Step 4: Publish & Optimize** (Daily, 30 min)
- Post at golden hours (11:30 AM, 7 PM weekdays; all day Sunday)
- Niche hashtags (#danhagiap #doanvathanglong)
- Reply to comments within first 2 hours
- Track completion rate → adjust hooks

## 4. What AI Can and Cannot Do (Critical Boundaries)

**AI IS GOOD at:**
- Script generation & idea brainstorming
- Trend identification & competitor analysis
- Video editing (captions, transitions, effects)
- Batch production of product showcase videos
- Hashtag strategy & posting optimization

**AI CANNOT replace:**
- ASMR food shots — needs real food, real sounds, real close-ups
- Kitchen transparency / BTS — people need to SEE your actual workspace
- Customer reactions — needs genuine human faces and emotions
- Livestream personality — real-time interaction builds trust

**Rule of thumb:** Use AI for 30–40% of workflow (research, scripting, editing). Core revenue-driving content must be real. Overly produced AI content performs poorly on TikTok Vietnam — raw authenticity converts better.

## 5. Prompt Templates (Vietnamese Context)

### Idea Generation:
> "Tôi là người mới bán đồ ăn vặt online tại Việt Nam, sản phẩm chính là [product]. Hãy phân tích các video snack viral trên TikTok và đề xuất 20 ý tưởng nội dung. Chia thành 4 nhóm: ASMR crunch, BTS nhà bếp, showcase combo, bắt trend."

### Script Writing:
> "Viết script 15 giây cho video ASMR [product]. Hook trong 3 giây đầu phải gây tò mò. Ngôn ngữ tiếng Việt tự nhiên, không giống quảng cáo. Nhấn mạnh [key selling point]."

### Trend Adaptation:
> "Tôi nghe thấy trend âm thanh [describe sound]. Hãy gợi ý cách áp dụng trend này cho kênh bán [product] của tôi. Video 10–15 giây, phong cách tự nhiên, không gượng ép."

## 6. Content Mix Framework (Proven Ratio)

| Type | Share | Purpose | Conversion |
|------|-------|---------|------------|
| Product ASMR/Crunch | 40% | Trigger impulse via sound/visuals | Highest |
| BTS / Kitchen transparency | 20% | Build trust (food safety) | Medium-High |
| Customer reaction / UGC | 20% | Social proof | Medium |
| Trend participation | 10% | Algorithm reach boost | Low but high reach |
| Educational / Recipe tips | 10% | Authority, saveable content | Low direct sales |

---

## 7. E-E-B Content Formula (Top Brands Use This)
60% **Entertain** → 30% **Educate** → 10% **Brand**

Examples:
- Entertain: ASMR crunch, humor, relatable scenarios
- Educate: Ingredient sourcing, food safety tips, storage advice
- Brand: Your story, your kitchen, your team values
